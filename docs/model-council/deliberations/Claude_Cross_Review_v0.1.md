Source: Claude web application, Opus 5 High, council run `MC-2026-09-05-001`.

Status: captured structured summary of a completed response ending `CLAUDE_CROSS_REVIEW_COMPLETE`. The live conversation URL is recorded in the council checkpoint. This is analysis, not certification, independent professional approval, or verified market research.

## Agreements

Claude agreed that:

- the full platform is not one two-week deliverable, while a bounded slice may be feasible if the harness, CI, oracles, reviewers, and topology are ready;
- the company must be described as AI-led and human-accountable;
- product/platform, service-delivery, and tokenization have distinct sales and liability surfaces despite sharing a control plane;
- permanent provider-brand roles violate provider neutrality;
- an independent control plane, canonical envelopes, external policy decisions, boundary enforcement, versioned registries, qualified adapters, fail-closed routing, immutable evidence, recovery, and tenant-isolated execution are the right architectural direction;
- a 48-hour production-intent experiment is the best way to convert estimates into measured readiness;
- USD 50M recognized revenue at month 24 is not credible from the present starting point, and USD 10M is a low-probability stretch;
- all revenue and valuation figures in the council are scenarios rather than evidence.

## Corrections and dissents

1. **Brand roles rejected.** Roles are capability and authority contracts. A provider/model/version/configuration may fill a role only under a current qualification record for the task class, domain, and risk tier.
2. **Founder-simulated independence rejected.** Keys, sessions, delays, and separate AI agents can decorrelate errors but cannot create independent authority or a second accountable natural person.
3. **Vacancy is honest but not assurance.** A vacant human-only office must block the relevant action and be disclosed as a capability gap; it cannot be described as independent review.
4. **Scope needs three labels.** Every component should distinguish release scope, validated scope, and exercised scope. A release-scope component that has not passed its gates must remain inert and visibly flagged.
5. **Review capacity is the bottleneck.** Agent availability does not remove human review, domain oracle, remediation, integration, or elapsed-test constraints.
6. **Wedge and platform are separate plans unless deliberately joined.** A service-revenue wedge and the regulated platform can share assets, but time and costs must be allocated explicitly.

## Segregation-of-duties resolution

Claude recommended:

- append-only, hash-linked and signed events with periodic anchoring to a location the founder cannot unilaterally rewrite;
- registered human-only offices that remain vacant and fail closed until filled;
- any founder override recorded as an event and propagated as a permanent `self_approved` attribute in downstream evidence;
- truthful customer-facing language that never claims independent review or four-eyes control for self-filled gates;
- a contracted external release/security reviewer before regulated-customer assurance is claimed;
- multi-provider adversarial review retained as `model-diversity assurance`, never counted as organizational independence.

The council adjudicator accepts the principle but rejects an unrestricted override. Some actions must be absolutely non-overridable by the founder or agents, including illegal actions, falsifying evidence, bypassing contractual independence, disabling immutable audit, unauthorized production release, and unauthorized movement of customer/payment data.

These are unsourced priors and must be replaced by experiment data:

| Slice | Ready prerequisites | Greenfield | Confidence stated |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Control plane only | 60–75% | 30–45% | Moderate |
| Control plane plus one non-regulated vertical | 40–55% | 20–30% | Moderate |
| Above plus one non-transactional ISO 20022 sandbox path | 20–35% overall | Not separately stated | Low–moderate |
| Full stated MVP scope in 14 days | Below 5% | Below 5% | High confidence in low result |

Claude recommended the control-plane slice and the 48-hour readiness experiment first. The council adjudicator corrects one framing issue: the founder's cited two-week target is a bounded ISO 20022 payment microservice release, not the whole company platform. Its probability therefore must be measured against the exact microservice profile and Day-0 readiness contract, not Claude's whole-MVP row.

## Freeze recommendations

- canonical versioned Task, Result, Event, and Decision envelopes;
- append-only registries for roles, policies, skills, standards, providers, tools, MCP servers, adapters, topologies, tenants, and qualifications;
- expiring qualification tuples bound to task class, domain, risk, provider, model version, evaluation suite, and evidence;
- policy decisions outside the executing agent and enforcement at every material boundary;
- fail-closed routing when authority, qualification, evidence, required office, or audit sink is missing;
- signed hash-linked audit events plus external anchoring;
- explicit human control offices and permanent self-approval disclosure;
- shared management plane with isolated execution/data planes;
- idempotency and replay-tested recovery;
- explicit AI authority exclusions;
- release/validated/exercised state labels;
- a portability rule for database-specific logic.

The adjudicator changes the last item from Claude's blanket ban on database-vendor-specific business logic to a contract: PL/SQL may be used where justified, but it must live behind versioned ports, have deterministic parity/contract tests, declare Oracle dependency, and prevent unsupported deployment profiles from being advertised.
