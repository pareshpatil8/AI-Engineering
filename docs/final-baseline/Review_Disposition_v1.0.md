## Remaining evidence and authority

These final documents are concrete specifications. They must not claim that all atomic skills are implemented, all controls are executed, all standards are applicable, or all four providers reviewed every new row. Source verification, operational role appointments, implementation, test execution and qualification produce those separate records. Jurisdictional/legal interpretation and restricted standards access remain accountable domain activities. Whole-catalog cross-reference checks and test results must be included in the package's final status register, with failures visible.
