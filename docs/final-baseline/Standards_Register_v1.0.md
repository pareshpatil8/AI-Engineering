# Standards Applicability Register v1.0

Status: consolidated register specification for founder review, 2026-09-05. All listed domains remain in the single MVP scope. A registered standard is not a conformance claim, license, certification or permission to operate. The register has 51 source/profile records. STDREG IDs are separate from legacy STD requirement IDs. These new entries have not received an item-by-item review by all external models.

## Applicability and control extraction contract

Each entry is a candidate triggered by entity, customer, geography, product, data, task and deployment. Package mappings route it for assessment; they do not declare every clause mandatory for every role. Legal duties, scheme/contract obligations, voluntary standards and informative guidance retain their distinct authority. ISO, SWIFT, OWASP and NIST are all first-release baseline families, with risk-specific applicability and equal change-control rigor.

Before activation, record the authoritative exact edition/revision, amendments/corrigenda, publication/effective/transition dates, licensed location or immutable snapshot/digest, source terms, owner, assessor, applicability decision, scope and evidence. Then extract each applicable clause into CTRL-<standard-id>-<local-id> with exact source reference, mandatory/advisory status, testable objective, implementation/enforcement point, parameters, evidence, positive and negative tests, reviewer and exception/expiry. A family-level record cannot substitute for these clause records.

An entry marked pending preserves a required acquisition/interpretation task. It cannot support an applicable compliance clearance until resolved. Not-applicable decisions need rationale, scope, evidence, reviewer, authorized owner and expiry. Scope additions trigger re-assessment. Licensed text must not be reproduced in public prompts or files beyond permission. Official public pages verified here establish only the stated edition/reference, not full clause review or licensing. Rows marked source_acquisition_pending are discovery pointers, not verified current legal guidance.

Review source changes monthly and before every regulated release/contract; urgent changes immediately trigger an impact proposal. Bind source changes to affected roles, skills, products, tenants and qualifications, evaluate effective dates, and requalify changed behavior. Never silently upgrade a production profile or overwrite historical evidence. Exceptions cannot waive law or a scheme/customer requirement without the authority that can lawfully change it.

## Priority acquisition and interpretation decisions

Before a regulated payment engagement, obtain the actual customer/participant NPCI and SWIFT packages, exact ISO20022 usage profiles, RBI applicable consolidated directions, legal data-processing/residency decision, DPDP phased commencement mapping, and independent assessor authority. Before management-system certification, obtain licensed ISO clauses and accredited assessment scope. Before high-accuracy security claims, pin ASVS requirement IDs and selected NIST controls and execute their tests. Before a CMMI maturity claim, obtain licensed model/appraisal criteria and measured organizational evidence. Public summaries and LLM consensus cannot resolve these gaps.

The scope includes cheque clearing, AML/fraud, KYC/asset tokenization, voice/customer service, third-party QA and cyber services. Those task profiles must expand family records into the exact triggered instruments; this document deliberately does not pretend one generic standard covers all products or all companies.
